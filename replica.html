About this replica

Building outlines follow aerial imagery, with roof elevations constrained by LiDAR. The eastern array uses the supplied photogrammetry and registered camera imagery.

Facades, parking canopies, vegetation and the southern array include modelled details. Uncaptured surfaces remain approximate. The perimeter is image-traced, not cadastral.

Normal shows modelled photovoltaic materials. W/T/Z shows the recorded eastern-array imagery on the same surface. Captures were made on 8 October 2026.
